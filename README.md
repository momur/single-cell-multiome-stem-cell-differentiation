
This repository contains analysis and figure-generation scripts
associated with our study of stem cell-derived pancreatic beta cell
differentiation using single-cell RNA-seq and ATAC-seq profiling.

The study integrates single-cell RNA-sequencing and chromatin
accessibility (scATAC-seq) data from Day 30 and Day 50 stem cell-derived
beta cells populations to characterize cellular heterogeneity,
developmental trajectories, and transcriptional regulatory programs.

## Analysis workflows

### Single-cell multiome preprocessing and integration

`day30_day50_preprocessing_and_integration.R`

Day 30 and Day 50 10x Genomics Single Cell Multiome datasets were
processed using Seurat and Signac. The workflow includes quality
control, RNA and ATAC processing, integration of the two developmental
time points, weighted-nearest-neighbor analysis, clustering, and
marker-based cell-type annotation.

### Trajectory and pseudotime analysis

`monocle_trajectory_analysis.R`

Monocle 2 was used to reconstruct developmental trajectories among beta
and enterochromaffin (EC) cell populations and to identify genes
associated with pseudotime and branch-dependent cell-state transitions.

### Gene regulatory network and in silico perturbation analysis

`celloracle_pipeline.py`

CellOracle was used to construct cell-type-specific gene regulatory
networks using single-cell expression and chromatin accessibility
information. The resulting regulatory models were used to simulate
transcription factor perturbations and predict their effects on
cell-state transitions.

### Regulon analysis

`pyscenic_pipeline.sh`

pySCENIC was used to infer transcription factor-target relationships,
identify motif-supported regulons, and quantify regulon activity using
AUCell.

## Figure generation

Scripts used to generate the manuscript figures are located in
`scripts/figure_generation/`.

These scripts use processed objects generated by the analysis workflows
above and reproduce the corresponding visualizations presented in the
manuscript.

## Data availability

Raw and processed single-cell multiome sequencing data associated with
this study will be deposited in the NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO).

The GEO accession number will be added upon release.

Large intermediate analysis objects are not included in this repository.

## Software and reproducibility

R package versions used for the analyses are recorded in `renv.lock`.

Major software packages used include Seurat, Signac, Monocle 2,
CellOracle, pySCENIC, chromVAR, SCENIC, and AUCell.

Analysis-specific parameters are documented within the corresponding
scripts.
